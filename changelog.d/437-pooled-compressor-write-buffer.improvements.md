* Lower GC pressure on binary inserts: the built-in compressors (`GZipCompressor`, `BrotliCompressor`, `Lz4Compressor`) no longer allocate a fresh 256 KiB `BufferedStream` buffer per batch on the large object heap. The buffering now rents its backing buffer from `ArrayPool<byte>`, eliminating one LOH allocation (and the Gen2 collection it drives) for every batch sent. Measured on a 500k-row insert (5 batches) into an `ENGINE Null` table, per-insert allocation drops from ~1.38 MB to ~135 KiB (≈90% less) across GZip, Brotli, and LZ4. Behavior is unchanged.
