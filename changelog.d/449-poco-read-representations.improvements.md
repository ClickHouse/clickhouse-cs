* `QueryAsync<T>` can now bind one column to more than one CLR type, chosen by the property: `DateTime`/`DateTime64`/`Date` into `DateTime`, `DateTimeOffset` or `DateOnly`; `String`/`FixedString` into `string` or `byte[]`; `Decimal` into `decimal` or `ClickHouseDecimal`; `Enum8`/`Enum16` into `string` (the label) or `int` (the stored numeric value); and, on .NET 8+, `Int128`/`UInt128` into the native `System.Int128`/`System.UInt128` as well as `BigInteger`. `MapTo<T>` keeps its strict exact-framework-type rules.
