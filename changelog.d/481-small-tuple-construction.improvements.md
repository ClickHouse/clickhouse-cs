* Faster reading of `Tuple(...)` columns with up to 7 elements: `TupleType.Read` now constructs the `System.Tuple<...>` directly through a cached compiled factory instead of allocating two intermediate `object[]` buffers and invoking the constructor reflectively via `Activator.CreateInstance`. Tuples with more than 7 elements keep the existing `LargeTuple` path. Behavior is unchanged, including the exact returned `System.Tuple<...>` CLR type (never a `ValueTuple`), `NULL`s inside `Nullable(...)` elements surfaced as `null`, and nested composite elements.
