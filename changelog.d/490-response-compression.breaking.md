* **If you set `AcceptEncoding` and then read a raw result, you now get compressed bytes.** `ExecuteRawResultAsync`, `PostStreamAsync` and `InsertRawStreamAsync` hand back the response body exactly as the server sent it. Previously `HttpClient` quietly decompressed `gzip` and `deflate` on the way through, so asking for `gzip` gave you plaintext; now it gives you gzip. Call `ClickHouseRawResult.ReadDecompressedStreamAsync()` to have the driver decompress it, or check `ContentEncoding` and decompress it yourself. Raw exports with no `AcceptEncoding` set are unaffected — plaintext before, plaintext now.
